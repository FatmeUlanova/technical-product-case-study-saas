# Product Roadmap

## Overview

The FlowSecure product roadmap outlines the phased development strategy for delivering a scalable SaaS platform that improves security monitoring and operational workflows.

The roadmap focuses on delivering value incrementally while validating assumptions through user feedback and real-world usage.

---

# Roadmap Strategy

The product will be delivered through three main phases:

1. Minimum Viable Product (MVP)
2. Workflow Expansion
3. Advanced Intelligence & Optimization

Each phase builds on the previous one to improve usability, automation, and operational insight.

---

# Phase 1: Minimum Viable Product (MVP)

## Objective

Deliver the core platform capabilities required for technical teams to centralize alerts and track incidents.

## Key Features

- Unified monitoring dashboard
- Alert severity categorization
- Basic incident workflow tracking
- Jira ticket integration
- Basic reporting dashboard

## Success Criteria

- Initial user adoption by technical teams
- Alerts successfully aggregated from monitoring tools
- Incidents tracked through structured workflows

---

# Phase 2: Workflow Expansion

## Objective

Improve operational efficiency through automation and deeper integrations.

## Key Features

- Workflow automation rules
- Slack and messaging integrations
- Alert context enrichment
- Advanced filtering and tagging
- Role-based dashboards

## Success Criteria

- Reduction in manual operational steps
- Increased workflow automation adoption
- Improved incident response coordination

---

# Phase 3: Advanced Intelligence & Optimization

## Objective

Introduce advanced analytics and intelligent prioritization to further improve operational efficiency.

## Key Features

- AI-assisted alert classification
- Predictive incident detection
- Advanced analytics dashboards
- Cross-team operational reporting
- Custom automation pipelines

## Success Criteria

- Significant reduction in alert fatigue
- Faster incident detection
- Improved operational visibility for leadership

---

# Product Milestones

| Phase | Milestone | Description |
|------|-----------|-------------|
| Phase 1 | MVP Launch | Core monitoring and incident management capabilities released |
| Phase 2 | Automation Release | Automation workflows and integrations added |
| Phase 3 | Intelligence Layer | Advanced analytics and predictive insights introduced |

---

# Product Development Principles

The roadmap follows several key product principles:

- Deliver value incrementally
- Prioritize high-impact features
- Validate assumptions with real users
- Maintain strong collaboration with engineering teams
- Continuously measure product performance

---

# Long-Term Vision

FlowSecure aims to become a central platform for operational monitoring and workflow coordination across technical organizations.

By combining monitoring, workflow management, and intelligent automation, the platform will allow teams to respond faster, collaborate better, and maintain reliable systems at scale.
